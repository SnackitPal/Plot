REAL DATA VS INTERNET NOISE

Why our data isn't just Reddit noise.

Most online polls get brigaded by 14-year-olds or bot accounts. Here is how we keep the numbers trustworthy.

🏛️

Real academic survey baselines.

Every dilemma is benchmarked against authentic probability-sampled research from the Pew Research Center and the World Values Survey. That gives us 24,500 verified real-world answers to measure community votes against.

⚖️

Math that cleans up small samples.

If a country only has 15 people voting, we do not let those 15 people skew the whole map. We blend smaller groups into wider regional baselines so the numbers remain honest and balanced.

🌍

True global balance.

Most English-speaking apps sound like a small slice of North America. PLOT splits votes across 19 world regions, giving equal weight to perspectives across Latin America, Africa, the Middle East, and Asia.

BEHIND THE PROJECT

Why we built this.

Most social feeds are built to make you think everyone is crazy or evil. When every conversation is flattened into an upvote war or angry quote dunks, we forget that different cultures have honest, understandable reasons for how they live.

A software engineer in Seattle, a civil servant in Tokyo, and a shopkeeper in Nairobi do not disagree because one is a genius and the others are villains. They disagree because they grew up with totally different trade-offs around personal freedom, family duties, and community expectations.

PLOT was built as an easy, fun way to explore those differences with genuine curiosity instead of anger.

"Human consensus is rarely unanimous, but it is rarely arbitrary." — The PLOT Charter
